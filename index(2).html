Multi-agent model of political polarization

Models agents with social networks who respond to policies and demographic change, as well as parties with factions, in attempt to recreate real world conditions and hopefully locate causes of polarization. Top bar contains presets that I've found are particularly interesting. Currently, factions don't really have that much of an impact, but I think they'll become important at some point.

Pressing a button switches the model to a preset that is supposed to do what the text on that button does (although the model is a bit grainy at the moment, so it's often a bit buggy). Examples include the polarization button, which turns down how much political difference people are willing to put up with, and the faction churn button, which increases the amount of internal change in parties.

The actors have families, homes, social networks, wealth, personal beliefs. The parties pass policies, and then the actors see how they're affected and decide how to change their political beliefs based on that.

Foreign policy not modeled, sorry.
